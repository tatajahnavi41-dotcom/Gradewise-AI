# 🎬 CampusIQ — 3-Minute Video Presentation Script

**Title:** CampusIQ: AI-Powered Student Analytics & Success Platform  
**Target Duration:** 3 Minutes (180 seconds)  
**Presenter:** Full-Stack & AI Engineering Lead  

---

## ⏱️ [00:00 - 00:30] Scene 1: The Problem & The Vision

**Visual:**  
Camera on presenter, transition to CampusIQ Login Page (`http://localhost:5173`) displaying the deep navy background with glowing cyan and indigo accents.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Traditional college analytics focus only on one thing: calculating final marks and identifying failing students when it's already too late. But what about recognizing medium-performing students who are improving? What about toppers who have great grades but zero placement readiness?*  
>  
> *Welcome to **CampusIQ — AI-Powered Student Analytics & Success Platform**. Our mission is to predict difficulties early, personalize roadmaps for every student, and recognize growth—not just topper marks."*

---

## ⏱️ [00:30 - 01:15] Scene 2: Role 1 — Campus Administrator Intelligence

**Visual:**  
Click the **"Campus Administrator"** 1-click judge button. The Campus Intelligence Dashboard loads with 180 students across AIML, AIDS, and CSE.

**Actions Demonstrated:**
1. Point out summary metrics: **180 Students**, **25 Staff Records**, Average Success Score **72.8/100**, and **42 Active Mentorship Interventions**.
2. Hover over the **Branch Success Comparison Bar Chart** (AIML, AIDS, CSE).
3. Open **Risk Intelligence Centre**: Show the independent breakdown between Academic Risk, Attendance Debarment Risk, and Placement Benchmark Risk.
4. Open **Student Segmentation**: Show the 7 dynamic diagnostic clusters (Balanced High Performers, High Academics Low Placement, Comeback Champions).

**Voiceover:**  
> *"As an Administrator, CampusIQ gives you a complete bird's-eye view of all 180 engineering students. Everything here is computed live from our local database—no mock totals.*  
>  
> *Notice our Risk Intelligence Centre. Instead of a black-box AI, CampusIQ uses explainable heuristics. A student flagged for attendance sees the statutory 60% university threshold, exact class hours missed, and an automated recommendation to protect them from exam debarment."*

---

## ⏱️ [01:15 - 01:55] Scene 3: Role 2 — Faculty Mentorship & Smart Matching

**Visual:**  
Use the top-right **Demo Switcher** to switch to **"Faculty: Dr. Rajesh Kulkarni (AIML HoD)"**.

**Actions Demonstrated:**
1. Show the branch-filtered view for AIML students.
2. Click **"Smart Mentor Matching"**: Select a struggling student from the dropdown. Show the algorithm scoring faculty mentors by specialization and workload capacity, then click **"Assign Mentor"**.
3. Open **"Update Marks"**: Enter internal marks for a student, save, and show the instant recalculation of their overall success score.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Faculty only see data for their assigned branch. Our standout innovation here is **Smart Mentor Matching**.*  
>  
> *When a student struggles in Machine Learning, the system matches them with Dr. Kulkarni because his specialization is Deep Learning and he currently has open mentee capacity. Faculty can record marks or attendance and the entire scoring engine recalculates instantaneously."*

---

## ⏱️ [01:55 - 02:40] Scene 4: Role 3 — Student Growth Journey & High-Impact Innovations

**Visual:**  
Switch to student persona: **"Student: Comeback Champion (AIML-015)"** or **"Student: Aarav Sharma (AIML-001)"**.

**Actions Demonstrated:**
1. Show the **Student Growth Journey**: Current Success Score, Class Rank #1, and the **Comeback Score** (+18 positive delta).
2. Click **"Audit Score Breakdown"**: Modal opens showing the explainable formula `(0.30×Academics) + (0.20×Attendance) + (0.15×LMS)...`.
3. Open **"Next Exam Difficulty Radar"**: Show the ML302 syllabus difficulty matched against the student's weak units.
4. Open **"Career Mission Mode"**: Show the pathway for *AI/ML Engineer* with skills progress bars and actionable tiers.
5. Open **"Gamified Learning Arena"**: Click on the Binary Search Tree diagram question, select the correct traversal `20, 30, 40, 50, 70`, click submit, and watch the celebratory confetti and +25 Growth Points!
6. Open **"What-If Simulator"**: Move the attendance slider from 62% to 85% to see real-time score surge without altering the official database.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Now for the student experience. Students are motivated rather than penalized. Notice the **Comeback Score**—it specifically rewards students who improve compared to their own previous semester.*  
>  
> *Students can inspect their **Next Exam Difficulty Radar**, track tier missions in **Career Mission Mode**, and practice visual questions in our **Gamified Learning Arena**.*  
>  
> *They can even test hypothetical scenarios in our **What-If Simulator** before taking their exams."*

> *Thank you, judges. CampusIQ is ready to empower every engineering student on campus."*
